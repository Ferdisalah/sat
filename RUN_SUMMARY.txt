METHOD
- Numeric bins are fold-specific quantiles fitted on the other four folds.
- Categorical bins retain up to the 12 most frequent reference-fold levels;
  rare levels are pooled as __OTHER__ and missing values are separate.
- TP-vs-FN compares distributions among actual defaults only.
- TP-vs-FP compares distributions among predicted defaults only.
- Total variation (TV) ranges from 0 to 1; higher means stronger separation.
- INTERACTION_RESIDUAL_LOG2 = joint-cell log2 lift minus the two marginal
  log2 lifts. It highlights joint enrichment/depletion beyond main effects.
  It is descriptive, approximate under smoothing, and not a fitted model term.
- BALANCED_RESIDUAL_SCORE balances the mean absolute TP/FN and TP/FP residuals.
  BALANCED_TV_SCORE is also retained, but can rise simply because a pair
  creates a finer partition than either feature alone.

INTERPRETATION CHECKLIST
1. Start with INTERACTION_SUMMARY.csv; inspect both TV columns, not only the rank.
2. Prefer candidates with TP-vs-FN separation that persists across folds.
3. Check TP-vs-FP separation beside it; a TP-vs-FN pattern alone may also
   describe false alarms and is not enough to justify a new feature.
4. Inspect INTERACTION_CELLS_POOLED.csv for the actual bin combinations,
   their TP/FN/FP/TN counts, default rates, precision, and smoothed log2 lifts.
5. Use INTERACTION_CELLS_BY_FOLD.csv and BIN_DEFINITIONS.csv to verify that
   the same percentile/category patterns persist and to recover real cut points.
6. Treat cells below the support flags cautiously. The script retains them
   in the CSVs but marks the minimum-support checks explicitly.
7. A large residual suggests a joint-bin pattern beyond the two marginal
   bin enrichments, but still needs replication and a controlled model test.

CAUTIONS
- This is post-hoc diagnostic analysis of the existing OOF model.
- High TV or interaction residual does not mean the feature improves AUC, AP, recall, or FPR.
- Group membership depends on the current model and its saved threshold.
- No causal interpretation is justified.
- Confirm a discovered pattern with a controlled, fold-preserving model
  experiment before claiming that a new interaction recovers FN safely.
